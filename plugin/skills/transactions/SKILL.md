---
name: transactions
description: See, add, change, delete, or restore the user's Clyde transactions, including payee, date, amount, note, tags, category, splits, and transfer links.
---

Find the user's transactions with `show_transactions` and change them with `edit`.

Details the user gives narrow which transactions: dates (such as "Jan 1 to 15"), an account's name, a category, or words in the payee or statement text. They may also say what to do, such as "split the charge on Jan 1 into $40 groceries and the rest household" or "add a $20 cash payment on Jan 1".

With no transaction named for a change, show the recent ones to pick from. With no change named, show the transactions the details point to.

When an `edit` answer lists matching Enhance rules, offer the user the rule so similar transactions are handled next time.
