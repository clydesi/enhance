---
name: schedule-e
description: Show a Clyde rental or other asset's Schedule E totals by tax line, for this year so far or a year the user names.
---

Show an asset's Schedule E with `show_schedule_e`.

An asset name the user gives picks the asset, and a year (such as "last year") picks the year. With no asset named, leave it out, and ask which one if the tool lists several. With no year named, leave it out.
