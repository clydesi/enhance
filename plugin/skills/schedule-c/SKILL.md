---
name: schedule-c
description: Show a Clyde business's Schedule C totals by tax line, for this year so far or a year the user names.
---

Show a business's Schedule C with `show_schedule_c`.

A business name the user gives picks the business, and a year (such as "last year") picks the year. With no business named, leave it out, and ask which one if the tool lists several. With no year named, leave it out.
