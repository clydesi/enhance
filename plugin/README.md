# Clyde

Intelligent personal finance in your AI app. This plugin pairs the Clyde connector with skills for the things you do most with your ledger.

## Use it

Type `/` in the message box and pick a Clyde skill, then add any details, such as "January" or "the charge on Jan 1". You can also just ask in your own words.

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| `ask` | Answers a question about your ledger. |
| `transactions` | Shows, adds, changes, or deletes transactions. |
| `accounts` | Shows balances, connects accounts, and adds or changes one. |
| `run-bookkeeping` | Works through new transactions and what needs a look, with you. |
| `transaction-audit` | Shows the transactions that may need a look. |
| `spending-report` | Shows spending by category for a month or a range of days. |
| `subscriptions` | Shows recurring charges and what they cost each month. |
| `schedule-c` | Shows a business's Schedule C. |
| `schedule-e` | Shows a rental or other asset's Schedule E. |
| `rules` | Shows, adds, changes, runs, or removes rules, including ready-made ones from Enhance. |
| `categories` | Shows, adds, or removes categories. |
| `businesses` | Shows, adds, changes, or removes businesses. |
| `import-file` | Brings in transactions from a file. |
| `settings` | Shows and changes your profile, plan, connected apps, passkeys, and connections. |

## Data

The plugin sends your requests to your Clyde account via the MCP connector. It stores nothing itself.
